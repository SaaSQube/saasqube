Our story

Three generations of home-style cooking

What began as a small family mess is now a warm, welcoming restaurant. We still grind our own masalas every morning, cook on slow flames, and serve every meal the way our grandmother taught us — generously.

From a quick breakfast to a celebration dinner for 40, we'd love to cook for you.

— The Saffron Leaf family
