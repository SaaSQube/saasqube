FAQ

Questions we hear often

How long does it take to build a website?

A Starter website is usually ready in about 7–10 working days once we have your content. Business sites take 2–4 weeks, and e-commerce stores 4–6 weeks depending on the number of products and integrations.

Can my website be in Tamil, Hindi or another Indian language?

Yes. We build sites in English, Tamil, Hindi and other Indian languages — fully in one language, or multilingual with a simple language switch — with proper fonts that read well on phones.

Do you provide domain, hosting and email?

Yes. We can register your .in or .com domain in your name, host the website with SSL, and set up professional business email like you@yourbusiness.in.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

If you'd like to, we build on an easy editor and train you. Or simply send us changes on WhatsApp and we'll handle them.

I'm not in Tamil Nadu. Can we still work together?

Absolutely. We work with businesses in every part of India — and abroad — entirely online over phone, video calls and WhatsApp. Our process, invoices (with GST) and support work the same wherever you are.

What are the payment terms?

Typically 50% to start and 50% before launch, with a proper GST invoice. Payment by UPI, bank transfer or cheque.
